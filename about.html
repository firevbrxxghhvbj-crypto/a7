Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, CleverStudyWay was established to champion active recall, evaluate spaced repetition algorithms, and provide an empirical guide for university students and lifelong learners.

Learning Science Mandate

Authentic Active Retrieval Over Ineffective Passive Highlighting

In an academic culture plagued by all-night cramming and passive textbook highlighting, CleverStudyWay advocates for self-testing active recall, Leitner box flashcards, and ultradian 50/10 focus intervals.

Our team of cognitive neuroscientists, academic productivity coaches, and memory researchers evaluate study techniques through rigorous laboratory trials: measuring memory retention curves, cognitive load limits, and sleep consolidation quality.

"Studying is not about the hours logged—it is about forcing active brain retrieval and timing review right before memory decay."

Academic Principles

Our Four Cognitive Study Standards

1. Active Recall

Testing memory retrieval strength using flashcards without looking at answers.

2. Spaced Repetition

Reviewing material right before memory decay occurs across 1, 3, 7, and 30-day intervals.

3. Deep Focus Intervals

Structuring 50-minute single-task deep work sprints with 10-minute restorative breaks.

4. Objective Research

100% independent study technique evaluations backed by cognitive neuroscience trials.
